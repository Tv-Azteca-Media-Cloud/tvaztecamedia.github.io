ADR-012 Accepted Core Layer
Bugfix and Hotfix Lines under Team-Capacity Constraints
TV Azteca · Salesforce Delivery Platform · ADP Whitelabel
Fecha: 2026-07-06 Estado: Accepted Layer: Core / Base Template
Context

By 2026-07-06 the project runs two release tracks in parallel: R1 is entering UAT stabilization (Go-Live 2026-07-25) and R2 is mid-flight (sprints S6–S8, Go-Live 2026-08-31). Two operational realities emerge that the main development model — one isolated sandbox per developer, artifact-based promotion — does not cover:

  1. Defects found in something already promoted must be fixed off the main sprint flow. A bug in what is deployed to SIT/UAT (R1) cannot wait for the next sprint's feature cycle; it is fixed on a dedicated bugfix line branched from develop-sit. A production incident (post Go-Live) needs an even faster path, branched from main.
  2. The team does not have the capacity to operate a fully robust, dedicated environment chain per line. The originally-anticipated hotfix chain (dedicated DEVPRD + QAPRD sandboxes, per RELEASE-CALENDAR.md milestones) will not be provisioned — there is no team bandwidth to develop, test, and maintain that line at full CI/CD robustness. Sandbox allotment is also finite: exactly one Full Copy (allocated to UAT) and one Partial Copy (allocated to HOTFIX). See ADR context in architecture/ENVIRONMENTS.md.

These two realities force two deliberate deviations from principles recorded in SOUL.md and architecture/CICD.md:

  • Shared developer sandbox for bugfixes. SOUL.md marks shared developer sandboxes as a non-negotiable anti-pattern ("shared dev sandboxes break CI, hide defects"). The bugfix lines use shared sandboxes (DEVR1 for R1-UAT bugfixes; the HOTFIX Partial Copy doubles as the construction+validation org for production hotfixes).
  • Delta (diff-based) deployment for production hotfixes. architecture/CICD.md marks diff-based deployment as an anti-pattern in favor of immutable-artifact promotion. An emergency production fix deploys only the delta of the change, not a regenerated full artifact.

The decision records why these exceptions are acceptable, bounded, and how the resulting risk is contained — so they are never silently adopted as the norm (SOUL non-negotiable #5).

Decision

Operate the R1 bugfix line and the production hotfix line as scoped, deliberate exceptions to the shared-sandbox and artifact-promotion principles — justified by team capacity and a bounded time window, with mandatory containment controls (backpromotion, dual approval, staged validation).

Mandatory containment controls

Because both exceptions weaken a safety net, the following controls are non-negotiable whenever a line is used:

  1. Backpromotion to develop is mandatory after every fix (R1 bugfix and PROD hotfix). No fix may exist only on its line.
  2. Reconciliation obligation with a deadline. A PROD hotfix backpromotes to develop immediately; develop-sit and the cumulative artifact (releases/rN) are reconciled no later than the next sprint's integration window. Until reconciled, the fix is absent from SIT — RM must not trigger a SIT deploy that would reintroduce the fixed defect. (See RM-RUNBOOK "Reconciliation Obligation".)
  3. Production hotfix requires dual approval — RM + TA. No single-person path to production.
  4. Staged validation before PROD. A hotfix is built in the HOTFIX Partial Copy and its deploy is rehearsed in PreProd before promotion to Production. The Partial Copy gives production-data parity for reproduction; PreProd gives a clean-org deploy rehearsal.
What this decision does NOT authorize
  • Shared sandboxes for feature development (the main model stands: one per developer).
  • Diff-based deployment for planned Major/Minor releases (artifact promotion stands).
  • Skipping backpromotion "because the release is close."
  • A production deploy on a single approval.
Consequences

Positive

  • The team operates within real capacity instead of maintaining an environment chain it cannot staff.
  • Emergency incidents have a defined, fast, still-governed path — the RM-RUNBOOK "Hotfix Path" stub is closed.
  • Exceptions are explicit and bounded, satisfying SOUL non-negotiable #5 (never resolve conflicts with principles silently).

Negative / risks accepted

  • Version-skew of the fix across environments until reconciliation completes. Mitigated by control #2 (deadline + RM gate on SIT deploys).
  • Reduced isolation on the shared bugfix sandbox. Mitigated by scope (surgical fixes only, low volume) and by the line being transient.
  • Delta deploy is non-deterministic by nature. Mitigated by restricting it to genuine emergencies and by mandatory backpromotion so the next artifact re-absorbs the change.
